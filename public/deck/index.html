The problem

Most content teams get hired backwards.

Wrong first seat

You hire hands before a head.

An editor joins first, output goes up, nothing lands. Two quarters later the real gap is obvious: you needed a producer with a point of view on what to make.

Not on the market

The good ones never apply.

India's strongest content leads are mid-IP at another brand. They don't sit on job boards or answer cold InMails. They get pulled, by someone they already trust.

Unscreenable on paper

A CV can't show craft.

Generalist recruiters filter on years and titles. Content roles are decided on reels, retention curves, hook rates and the IPs a person actually shipped.

The cost isn't the hiring fee. It's two quarters of content that didn't work.
